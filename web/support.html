Simple dinner ideas when you need one.

Dinner Inspiration picks from a built-in list of family dinner ideas. It works offline after installation and does not require an account.

How to use the app

  1. Open the app to see a dinner idea with an image, short description, main ingredients, and serving note.
  2. Tap Next to get another dinner idea.
  3. Tap Back to return to the previous idea.
  4. Swipe left or right on the dinner card to move between ideas.
  5. Tap the Search button to search the web for recipes related to the current dinner.

Language

The app supports English, Swedish, Norwegian, and Polish. Use the language menu in the toolbar to change the app language. Your choice is saved on this device.

Privacy

Dinner Inspiration does not use an account, upload your dinner choices, or run a backend service. Recipe searches open in your browser and are handled by the search provider.
